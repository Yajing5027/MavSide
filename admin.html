About this metric

MavSide Campus Access Subsidy pool operates on strict integer-cent conservation: Starting Reserve + Commissions (20% of 12%) + Helper Donations − Aid Outflows (MavPoints at 10¢/pt + Cash Aid) = Reconciled Ending Balance. Phantom cents are strictly detected with zero tolerance.

Financial Model Sensitivity & Break-Even Truth:

Platform charges 12% commission on delivery tasks, transferring 20% to the Impact Fund ($0.12/task). MavPoints ($0.10/pt) are fully funded liabilities alongside cash subsidies. If ordinary delivery volume drops or assisted requests surge, monthly coverage reverses. Full operational overhead (hosting, insurance, support, staff) is NOT ESTABLISHED in this prototype.
