Assistance request

Request a $2.00 assisted delivery to see how points and the subsidy pool cover the fee.

Rules: 30 MavPoints granted monthly to eligible users (once per month); 1 point = $0.10 off. In this campus finance model, the platform takes a 12% commission on eligible delivery/service fees (not merchandise prices), and 20% of that commission is donated to the Campus Access Subsidy pool. Points reduce the fee first, the pool covers the remainder, then the student pays. Request a delivery twice: the first uses points, the second shows the subsidy pool covering part of the fee.
