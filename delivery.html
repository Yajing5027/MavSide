Estimate details

Dashed lines show indoor paths; solid lines show outdoor footways. Indoor passages follow modeled hallway connections aligned to university plans. Outdoor routes use OpenStreetMap footways and short estimated doorway links. Building entrances and tunnel positions are approximate, not surveyed or certified routes. Hollow markers show locations without a modeled connection. Doorway and within-building access segments are approximate. Outdoor only excludes indoor travel between buildings; walking from the origin point to its door and from the destination door to its point is included and shown as indoor access. The straight-line distance is only a numerical fastest-time lower bound and is not drawn as a walkable line.

Times use a 1.4 m/s walking model, then add one 1–2 min allowance for the whole trip. Task estimates also include 90 s for pickup. These are planning estimates, not certified accessible routes.

Select a task row to view its route. Purple is your route; pink includes pickup and delivery. The 15% limit uses distance, not the time allowance.
